# Delete Your FindMyPart Account Last Updated: July 2026 FindMyPart allows users to request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data. ## How to Request Account Deletion To request deletion of your account: 1. Email us at **findmypartsupport@gmail.com 2. Use the subject line: **Delete My FindMyPart Account**. 3. Include the mobile number registered with your FindMyPart account. ## What Will Be Deleted When your request is processed, we will permanently delete: * User profile * Phone number * Personal information * Chat history * Spare part listings * Uploaded images and videos * Notifications * Part requests ## What May Be Retained We may retain certain information for legal, tax, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory compliance where required by applicable law. ## Processing Time Account deletion requests are normally processed within **7 business days**.
